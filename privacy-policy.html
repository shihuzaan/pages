Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

3D Screen Mirroring is designed to mirror your screen without collecting your personal information.

Information we collect

3D Screen Mirroring does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not use tracking, analytics, advertising identifiers, cookies, or a backend service.

Screen mirroring

When you choose to mirror your screen, the content is processed on your device and sent only to the display or receiver you select. The independent developer does not receive, view, or store mirrored content.

Permissions

3D Screen Mirroring may ask for macOS permissions needed to provide screen mirroring. These permissions are managed by macOS and can be reviewed or changed in your Mac’s System Settings.

Third-party services

3D Screen Mirroring does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-sharing services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
