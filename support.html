Before you get in touch

  • Check that your Mac and display are connected to the same network, where required.
  • Make sure MirrorMac has the screen permissions it needs in macOS System Settings.
  • Restart MirrorMac and try connecting again.
  • Update macOS and MirrorMac to their latest available versions.
MirrorMac does not collect diagnostic data automatically. Any details you send to [SUPPORT_EMAIL] are used only to respond to your support request.
