3D Screen Mirroring support

Need a hand with screen mirroring? We’re here to help.

Send us a short description of what happened, which Mac you are using, and the version of macOS installed.

screen_mirroring_app@outlook.com ↗

Before you get in touch

  • Check that your Mac and display are connected to the same network, where required.
  • Make sure 3D Screen Mirroring has the screen permissions it needs in macOS System Settings.
  • Restart 3D Screen Mirroring and try connecting again.
  • Update macOS and 3D Screen Mirroring to their latest available versions.
3D Screen Mirroring does not collect diagnostic data automatically. Any details you send to screen_mirroring_app@outlook.com are used only to respond to your support request.
